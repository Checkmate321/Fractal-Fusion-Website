What makes a good entry

  • Put the result in the summary. "Cycle time down 0.6 seconds" beats "we worked on the intake".
  • Numbers beat adjectives. "One jam in thirty cycles" says more than "much better".
  • Write down what failed. That is the part other teams actually learn from, and the part judges ask about.

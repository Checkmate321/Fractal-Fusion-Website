What you get

What a partnership delivers

Brand visibility where engineers are

Regional, state and international competitions, in front of thousands of attendees. The FIRST Championship alone draws more than 50,000 people from 66 countries. The audience is engineers, educators and industry professionals, not a general crowd.

Logo placement

On the robot, on team uniforms, on the pit display, and on every piece of competition material we produce.

Recognition all season

Across our social channels and on this website, not just once at signing. Our sponsor wall is part of the site rather than a page nobody updates.

A documented STEM connection

Everything we do is dated and public in the build log, so your support maps to specific work rather than a logo on a shirt. That makes it usable in ESG reporting and community investment narratives.

Regular performance updates

Results as the season runs, with your brand alongside them, and a written account of what your money actually paid for.

Not all of it has to be money

Materials and machine time count. Aluminium stock, polycarbonate, fasteners, bearings, filament. An engineer for one design review is worth more than most cheques, and that route lives on the partners page.

Where it goes

What a season actually costs

Every dollar goes through the team budget and is spent on one of these:

  • Registration and event fees. Team registration, league play, the state championship, and the World Championship when we earn it.
  • The robot. Motors, servos, the control system, structure, and the parts that break in week three.
  • Tools and workspace. What we build with, and what stays behind for the teams who come after us.
  • Travel. Getting a team and a robot to competition.
  • Outreach. Demonstration events, and the parts we hand to rookie teams getting started.

Named tiers with specific amounts arrive once this season's budget is finalised.

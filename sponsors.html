Why it matters

Two seasons, and a robot at the World Championship

Team 27188 started in 2024 knowing very little. In our second season we won our league tournament outright, were the first pick of the winning alliance at the Florida Championship, and competed in the Ross Division at the FIRST World Championship.

None of that is free. An FTC season costs real money in registration, parts, tools and travel, and the students running the team are the ones who have to find it. Sponsorship is the difference between a season spent building and a season spent fundraising.

Three ways to support us

The most flexible kind of help. It covers registration, competition parts, spares that fail mid season, and getting the team to events.

Two

Three

An engineer for one design review is worth more than most cheques. That route lives on the partners page.

Where it goes

What a season actually costs

Every dollar goes through the team budget and is spent on one of these:

  • Registration and event fees. Team registration, league play, and the state championship.
  • The robot. Motors, servos, the control system, structure, and the parts that break in week three.
  • Tools and workspace. What we build with, and what stays behind for the teams who come after us.
  • Travel. Getting a team and a robot to competition, including the World Championship when we earn it.
  • Outreach. Demonstration events, and the parts we hand to rookie teams getting started.

Named tiers with specific amounts arrive once this season's budget is finalised.
